# NVIDIA Valuation Report

## 1. Methodology
This report evaluates NVIDIA’s intrinsic valuation using a **Discounted Cash Flow (DCF)** framework alongside relative valuation multiples (P/E and EV/EBITDA).

## 2. Core Model Assumptions
- **Long-term Growth:** Gradual normalization of growth rates as global cloud infrastructure buildouts mature.
- **Terminal Growth Rate (g):** 3.5%
- **WACC:** Estimated at 9.5% to account for high-growth tech sector volatility and equity risk premiums.

## 3. Conclusion
NVIDIA's dominant market share in accelerated computing and deep software integration (CUDA) support a strong long-term valuation outlook, backed by robust operational cash flows.
