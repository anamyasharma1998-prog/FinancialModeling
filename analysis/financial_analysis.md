# NVIDIA (NVDA) Financial Analysis

## 1. Executive Summary
NVIDIA has transformed from a gaming GPU designer into the definitive hardware and software ecosystem supporting artificial intelligence worldwide. Its revenue growth velocity over recent fiscal years reflects unprecedented enterprise and cloud provider demand for generative AI training and inference.

## 2. Growth Drivers & Segment Performance
- **Data Center Expansion:** Data Center revenue scaled dramatically to $115.2 billion in FY2025, fueled by high demand for Hopper clusters and the rollout of the Blackwell architecture.
- **Pricing Power:** High technological moats allow the company to preserve strong gross margins despite massive scaling in global supply chains.

## 3. Cash Flow Dynamics
NVIDIA generates immense operating cash flow, comfortably funding extensive R&D initiatives while aggressively returning capital to shareholders through stock buybacks and dividends.
